# IdkRx

Report medication availability at US pharmacies from a conversation with Claude, and, for pharmacy staff, keep your pharmacy's IdkRx page current.

## Use it

Tell Claude what you saw, for example "the Walgreens on Glenwood in Raleigh is out of 10mg Adderall". Claude finds the pharmacy and the medication on IdkRx and opens a short form naming both; nothing is filed until you submit it.

If you work at a pharmacy with an IdkRx rep plan, you can also ask Claude to mark a medication your pharmacy tracks as available, limited or out of stock, or to post, change or clear the notice on your pharmacy's page. Claude shows a preview of every change and makes it only after you approve.

Sign in with your IdkRx account when you connect the IdkRx connector.

## Data

The plugin sends the pharmacy, medication and status you report, and any notice text you post, to your IdkRx account through https://mcp.idkrx.com. Reports and notices are shown publicly on IdkRx. The plugin stores nothing itself.
